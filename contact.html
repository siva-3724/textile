FAQ

Questions? We Have Answers.

Do you offer wholesale orders?

Yes. We welcome wholesale, bulk, boutique, retail, and business enquiries. Contact our team with your product requirements and expected quantity.

Can I request a specific design or fabric?

You can share your preferred fabric, colour, pattern, design, or reference image. Our team will review your requirement and advise you on available options.

Do you provide bulk pricing?

Bulk pricing may vary depending on the product, quantity, and requirements. Contact our wholesale team for a quotation.

Can boutiques partner with Avenza Textiles?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from boutiques, retailers, designers, and textile businesses interested in building long-term partnerships.

Can I visit the showroom?

Absolutely. Customers and business partners are welcome to visit our showroom during our regular working hours.

How can I check product availability?

Send us the product name, collection, or reference you're interested in and our team will assist you with availability.
